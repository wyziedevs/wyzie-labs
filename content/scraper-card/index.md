## Overview

Made this because I got tired of using old cards in my wallet to scrape prints off the bed. It's only about as wide as two credit cards stacked together, so it's not quite bulky. The thin beveled edge gets under stuff without scratching anything up, and the wide handle gives you actual grip when a print is really stuck on there. 

Also great for chopping lines if that's your thing, no judgment from me. Fits in your wallet or just toss it in a drawer somewhere.

**Note:** each notch is 0.2 inches apart so you can easily measure in small increments, the card is 3.375 inches long and 2.125 inches tall with a thickness of 0.08 inches.
